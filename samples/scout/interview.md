# Setup interview

Answers to the questions Job Scout's setup asks before the first scan (see SKILL.md, Setup step 2).
`python run.py profile-from-resume samples/scout/resume.txt --interview samples/scout/interview.md`
turns this and the resume into a profile. Where the two disagree, these answers win.

## What are you looking for?
Staff, Principal, Group or Lead Product Manager. Senior PM is fine at a strong company; rank it
below Staff. Director of Product at a small or mid-size company. AI product, platform, or
internal AI enablement work.

## Where?
Fully remote in the US, or hybrid in the Boston area at two or three days a week. Home base is
Scituate, MA, on the South Shore: Boston, Cambridge, Quincy, Braintree, Waltham, Needham and
Burlington are all commutable. Five days a week in an office is a no.

## Compensation?
Base floor $180,000. Target $220,000 or more. A posting with no salary listed is fine.

## What should never reach your inbox?
Associate or junior PM roles. Product owner roles that are ticket-writing with no strategy.
Product marketing, program or project management, sales engineering. Staffing and recruiting
firms reposting other companies' jobs.

## What makes a role a strong fit?
Owning a product area end to end: strategy, discovery, roadmap and outcomes. AI/ML products,
especially output quality, evals, and model and context choices. Platform or internal tooling
where the users are other teams. 0-to-1 work with real scope.

## Anything a screener should be skeptical of?
Postings that use AI vocabulary for a role that is really backlog management. Onsite-only roles
outside Massachusetts.
